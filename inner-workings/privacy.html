Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

"Inner Workings" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Inner Workings YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, the tool uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload and schedule videos to the owner's own channel and edit their titles, descriptions, tags and visibility;
  • read the owner's own channel and video statistics (views, average percentage viewed, likes and subscribers gained) through the YouTube Analytics API.

What is stored

The OAuth credentials are kept as private environment settings in the owner's own build environment, and are never published or shared. Aggregate statistics for the channel's own videos are kept in the owner's private project files. The tool collects no data about viewers or any other YouTube users, uses no cookies, and does not sell, share or transfer any data.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from Google account permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads and statistics collection.

Third-party terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Contact the channel owner through the Inner Workings YouTube channel's About page.
